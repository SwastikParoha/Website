How to use the Blue PDRN Face Serum

Take 2 to 3 drops on clean, dry skin and massage until it absorbs. Follow with moisturizer, and with sunscreen in the daytime.

How to use the Blue PDRN Face Moisturizer

Take a small amount and apply on clean, dry face and neck. Massage until absorbed. Use it morning and evening, or whenever your skin feels dry.

How to use the Face Wash

Work a small amount into a lather on damp skin, massage for 30 seconds and rinse. Good for oily and acne-prone skin, gentle enough for every skin type.

How to use the Advanced Anti-Ageing Cream

One pump on clean skin at night. Anti-ageing skincare works slowly, so use it regularly and give it time. Wear sunscreen the next morning.

How to use the Ultralight Fluid Sunscreen

Apply generously as the last step of your morning routine, on face and neck. Reapply after long hours outdoors.
