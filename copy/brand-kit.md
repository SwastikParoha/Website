# Contemprory — Brand kit

Source: the current site (contemprory.com), read from ten screenshots in
`assets/references/site-screens/`. Anything marked **[placeholder]** was not
visible on the site and must be confirmed before launch.

## 1. Brand name
**Contemprory**. The wordmark is a heavy black "C" ring holding the
lowercase "ontemprory", with "Define Your Presence" set small underneath.
The spelling "Contemprory" is the brand's own; never "correct" it.

## 2. Slogan
**Define Your Presence.**

## 3. Positioning
Ingredient-led, dermatology-style skincare for India at accessible prices
(₹699–₹1,499). Each product leads with its actives on the pack
(PDRN, copper tripeptide, retinal, bakuchiol, niacinamide, salicylic acid,
ectoin). The brand is cleaner and calmer than the loud "glow" brands and more
affordable than imported clinical labels: modern science, plain language,
a five-step routine you can actually keep.

## 4. Target audience
Indian women and men, roughly 20–40, in cities, who read ingredient lists,
follow skincare creators, and want results without a twelve-step routine.
Many have oily or acne-prone skin and deal with sun, heat and pollution
daily. Some are starting with anti-ageing for the first time.

## 5. Brand personality
Clean, confident, modern, scientific, approachable, honest, fresh, calm.
It sounds like a friend who knows the science and explains it in one
sentence, then tells you to be patient because good skin takes time.

## 6. Color palette
Light, airy, sky-and-water. Taken from the current site's photography and UI.

| Role | Hex | Where it comes from / how it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#F3F7FC` | a sky-tinted white; the page canvas (never pure white) |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` at 70% over the bg, or `#FAFCFF` | cards, the nav bar, the 3D stage floor |
| Mist | `#DCE7F2` | the sunscreen and PDRN backdrops; soft section fills |
| Sky | `#8CB4E6` | the hero sky; large calm color fields, secondary light |
| Text (ink) | `#0F1724` | headlines, body, primary buttons (the site's navy-black "Buy Now") |
| Muted text | `#5A6678` | descriptions, sizes, secondary copy |
| Primary accent: Cobalt | `#3B47D6` | the Blue PDRN serum glass; CTAs, highlights, rim light, the 3D liquid |
| Secondary accent: Apricot | `#EBC6A3` | the site's headline highlight band; warm rim light, small highlights |
| Line | `#D3DDEA` | borders, dividers, hotspot leader lines |

The cobalt is the one strong accent. Apricot is a warm counterpoint, used
rarely. The 3D scene's rim lights are cobalt and apricot; the particles are
soft sky-white "dew".

## 7. Typography
- **Headings: Bricolage Grotesque** (Google Fonts), weights 600 and 800.
  A bold grotesk with character, close to the heavy tight headlines on the
  current site ("PURE SCIENCE IN EVERY DROP") but more distinctive.
- **Body: DM Sans** (Google Fonts), weights 400 and 500. Matches the light,
  round body and callout text on the current site.

## 8. Logo direction
Keep the existing wordmark: a thick black open "C" ring with "ontemprory"
set inside it in a rounded lowercase script-sans, "Define Your Presence"
beneath. On the site it is recreated as inline SVG/HTML (the C ring as an
SVG arc, the word as live text), never as a raster image. Favicon: the C
ring alone in ink on the sky-tinted background.

## 11. Visual mood

dark. Materials: frosted and cobalt glass, polished silver collars, glossy
serum drops, water droplets beading on surfaces, satin, a centella leaf.

apricot rim on the other. Atmosphere: floating dew and tiny light motes
(the sparkle field from the current hero), drifting slowly upward like
bubbles in serum. Camera: slow, close, macro-friendly, product-photography
calm.

## 17. Design bans
- No cream or off-white page background.
- No italic accent words inside headlines.
- No numbered "01 / 02 / 03" section labels.
- No monospace eyebrow labels.
- No pill-shaped buttons.
- No gradient-filled headline text.
- No emoji as icons.
- No three identical feature cards in a row.
- No purple-to-blue gradients.
- No glass effect on every card.
- No dark theme or dark sections; the darkest block is a single ink CTA band.
- No real third-party brands or logos.
- No faded "ghost" of the 3D product showing through text sections (stage dims to 94% behind content).
- No hotspot labels running off the screen edge (labels flip to the left near the right edge).
- No cartoon-opaque serum drops: drops are clear, glassy, small.

## 18. Experience
**Option A, real 3D product, built in code.** No Higgsfield or other paid
generator: the Blue PDRN Face Serum bottle is modelled procedurally in
three.js (`website/src/bottle.js`): cobalt glass, white C-ring print, ribbed
silver collar, tall silver cap. The scene is high-key: a CSS sky gradient
behind a transparent WebGL canvas, a still-water floor with slow ripples,
clear serum drops hanging in the air, rising dew, a cobalt rim and an
apricot rim light. The five beats from section 15 are camera keyframes in
`website/src/main.js`. Explore offers three light modes: Morning sky, Clear
water, Golden hour. The poster (`website/public/img/hero.jpg`) is rendered
from beat 1 of the scene itself. Lower sections use the brand's own photos,
cropped from the current site.
